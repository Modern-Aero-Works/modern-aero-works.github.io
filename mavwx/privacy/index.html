MavWx Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Modern Aero Works ("we," "us," or "our") develops and operates MavWx, an aviation weather application for iPhone.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and shared when you use MavWx or contact our support team.

1. Information We Collect

MavWx does not require users to create a Modern Aero Works account to access the application.

MavWx does not integrate third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or user-tracking services.

The application may process technical information necessary to retrieve weather observations, manage subscriptions, and maintain application functionality.

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address, message contents, and any additional information you voluntarily provide.

2. Aviation Weather Information

MavWx retrieves aviation and local weather information from external data sources, including:

  • KLAS Digital ATIS information
  • Grand Canyon West Airport AWOS (1G4)
  • Red Rock / Blue Diamond Ridge weather information

To retrieve this information, your device communicates with external services over the internet.

These services may receive standard network information, such as your device's IP address, request time, and technical request details. Their handling of that information is governed by their own practices and applicable policies.

3. Subscription Information

MavWx offers an auto-renewable subscription through Apple's App Store.

Purchases, billing, subscription management, and payment processing are handled by Apple.

MavWx uses Apple's StoreKit functionality to verify subscription status and determine access to subscription features.

Subscription entitlement information, including relevant expiration and verification dates, may be stored locally on your device.

Modern Aero Works does not receive your full payment card details through the application.

For information about Apple's privacy practices, visit Apple's Privacy Policy .

4. Information Stored on Your Device

MavWx may store previously retrieved weather observations and subscription entitlement information on your device.

This information supports application functionality and information sharing between the main application and its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Local information may remain on your device until it is updated, cleared, or removed through normal application or operating system behavior.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

MavWx does not integrate advertising networks or third-party behavioral analytics services.

MavWx does not integrate a third-party crash-reporting SDK.

We do not use the application to track users across other companies' applications or websites for advertising purposes.

Apple may independently process diagnostic or App Store information under its own privacy practices and user settings.

6. Customer Support Communications

If you email our support team, we use the information you provide to respond to your request, investigate reported problems, and provide assistance.

Support correspondence may be processed by our email service provider and retained as reasonably necessary for support, recordkeeping, or applicable legal obligations.

Please do not include passwords, payment card numbers, or other sensitive information in support requests.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information collected through MavWx.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to deliver weather information, process App Store subscriptions, or respond to support requests.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect legal rights and application security.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information processed by MavWx and to use platform-provided mechanisms for subscription verification and local storage.

No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

MavWx is designed as an aviation weather utility and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an in-app account registration or advertising system.

If you have a concern about information provided through a support request, please contact us.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You may stop using MavWx and uninstall the application at any time.

You may manage or cancel an active subscription through your Apple Account settings. Uninstalling the application does not automatically cancel a subscription.

To request information about support correspondence or ask for deletion of information we hold in connection with your support request, contact us using the email address below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
