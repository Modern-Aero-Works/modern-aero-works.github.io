Frequently Asked Questions

What weather information does MavWx provide?

MavWx provides weather information from KLAS Digital ATIS, Grand Canyon West Airport AWOS (1G4), and the Red Rock reporting station. Available information depends on each source.

Why isn't my weather information updating?

Check your internet connection and reopen MavWx. Weather reports depend on external data providers and may occasionally be delayed or unavailable.

How often do widgets update?

MavWx requests periodic widget updates. Actual refresh timing is controlled by iOS and may vary with device conditions, network connectivity, and system scheduling.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open MavWx and select Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Make sure you're using the Apple Account associated with your original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, select MavWx, and follow the cancellation instructions.

You can also visit Apple's subscription cancellation guide .

How much does MavWx cost?

MavWx offers an annual auto-renewable subscription priced at $4.99 per year in the United States, with a three-day introductory free trial for eligible users. Your actual price and eligibility are displayed before purchase.

Can I use MavWx without internet access?

Previously retrieved weather information may remain visible temporarily, but current weather updates require access to the relevant data sources. Always verify the observation timestamp.
