Weather Dashboards

Three sources. One application.

KLAS Digital ATIS

View available digital ATIS information for Harry Reid International Airport in a structured, readable format.

1G4 AWOS

Access automated weather observations for Grand Canyon West Airport, including wind, altimeter, visibility, and other available reported conditions.

Red Rock Weather

View weather information from the Blue Diamond Ridge reporting station in a dedicated dashboard.

iPhone Widgets

Weather at a glance.

MavWx supports Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, providing convenient access to weather information without opening the full application.

Widget information depends on available station reports, network connectivity, and iOS refresh scheduling.

Annual Membership

One subscription. All dashboards.

MavWx offers an annual auto-renewable subscription for $4.99 per year in the United States, with a three-day free introductory trial for eligible customers.

Pricing, trial eligibility, and applicable terms are confirmed in Apple's purchase interface. Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled.

Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.
